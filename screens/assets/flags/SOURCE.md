# Country flag atlas

`atlas.png` is generated by `scripts/generate_flag_atlas.go` from the PNG
renditions served by https://flagcdn.com and ordered by ISO 3166-1 alpha-2
code. The artwork comes from the MIT-licensed Flag Icons project:
https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons

The atlas is embedded in the executable and is used at 17 × 12 pixels in the
FT8 activity viewer.

`dxcc-flags.json` maps active DXCC entity names to the ISO flag cells in the
atlas. It is generated by `scripts/generate_dxcc_flags.go` from the
Apache-2.0-licensed DXCC reference at:
https://github.com/k0swe/dxcc-json
